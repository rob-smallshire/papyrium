# papyrium.font.draft_9

draft-9 is a 9-pin dot-matrix font designed for Papyrium in the style of
1980s draft-quality printing. It is an original design, not a copy of any
printer's character ROM. Glyphs are 11 columns by 9 rows: capitals 7 dots
high, a lowercase x-height of 5 dots, and 2 rows of descender. As on a real
9-pin head, no pin strikes in two successive columns.

It covers printable ASCII and the characters of the Epson international
character sets (such as the pound sign, accented letters and the German
sharp s). The glyphs are in `glyphs.txt`, in the Papyrium dot font text
format, and can be edited there.

Registered as `draft-9` on the `papyrium.font` axis.
